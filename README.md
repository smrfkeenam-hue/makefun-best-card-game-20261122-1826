Ember Duel — a card battler in four elements against Cinder, the Ash Warden. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the best card game" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
npx serve game
```

Then open the printed address in desktop Chrome. No build step, no network: every file the game loads sits under game/.

## How it plays

You start at 30 life, Cinder at 34 — you move first each round, and the extra life balances that edge. Each turn you gain 3 ember and draw a card, then play ember-cost cards from your hand: Flame hits hard and burns, Frost hits and shields, Gale hits and draws or feeds you ember, Stone hits and walls up. Play a second card of the same element in one turn and it resonates — +2 damage. From turn 10 the table itself burns, so no duel drags on. Click a card or press 1–7 to play it, Space ends your turn, Enter starts, R replays.
