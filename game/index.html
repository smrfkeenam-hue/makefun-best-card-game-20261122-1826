EMBER DUEL

Four elements. One table. Cinder, the Ash Warden, waits.

Play ember-cost cards against Cinder. You start at 30 life, Cinder at 34 — you move first each round, and the extra life balances that. Each turn you gain 3 ember and draw a card.

Resonance: play a second card of the same element in one turn and it hits +2. From turn 10 the table itself burns — both sides take damage every turn, so no duel drags on.

Click a card or press 1–7 to play it. Space ends your turn. Enter starts. R replays after a duel ends.

Flame — pure damage, some burn. Frost — damage plus shield. Gale — damage plus draw or ember. Stone — damage plus heavy shield.

Your deck holds 24 cards: two copies of each 1- and 2-cost card, one of each 3- and 4-cost card, in every element.

Victory — Cinder falls!

The Ash Warden's embers scatter across the table. The duel is yours.

Defeat — Cinder stands

The table goes dark. Shuffle up and try a new hand.
